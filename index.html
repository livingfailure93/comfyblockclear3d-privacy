Comfy Block Clear 3D: Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Comfy Block Clear 3D (the "Game") handles and why. The Game is published on Google Play under the developer account livingexcellencegames by 33EXE STUDIOS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us").

In short: the Game has no ads and no in-app purchases. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location, and we never sell or share your information.

1. Information kept on your device

Your progress (levels reached, stars and play counts) and your settings (sound, music, vibration and graphics) are saved on your phone. They stay there and are removed when you uninstall the Game.

2. Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides the Game with your Play Games player ID and display name so that cloud saves work. Your progress can be stored in your Google account's saved games so it follows you to a new phone. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play the whole Game without signing in.

3. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad networks and no advertising ID use.
  • No in-app purchases or payment information.
  • No analytics or tracking of how you play.
  • No selling, renting or trading of any information.
  • No collection of your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

4. Children

The Game's content is family-friendly, but the Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Security and where data is stored

Saved games kept through Google Play Games are stored on Google's servers, which may be outside your country, and are protected by Google's security measures. Data sent by the Game is encrypted in transit.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Uninstalling the Game removes everything stored on your phone.
  • You can remove the Game's Play Games data from your Google account at any time (Play Games app or myaccount.google.com).
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU GDPR), you may have further rights, and we will honour them. Email us with any request.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example if a later version adds analytics or crash reports, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's update notes.
